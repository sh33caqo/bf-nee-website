Global trends

From gross natural ecosystem loss to the consumption-attributed footprint

Plantations no longer count as natural (v3.1, approximation). Plantations (IUCN 14.3) and artificial aquatic classes are now treated as anthropogenic. Until the full pipeline can be rerun, natural ecosystem loss and BF-NEE are derived from the v3 results by removing these classes; the exact values lie between the values shown and the v3 values (Method). The interactive map still shows the v3 values.

All figures sum over the 151 producing countries that pass the data checks.

From loss to footprint

Three steps narrow the observed loss down to the footprint: gross natural loss (unconstrained), the part that coincides with agricultural expansion in the same cell (agriculture-constrained), and finally the share of it that is linked to German final demand (BF-NEE).
